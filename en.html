Desk Bookfair


Desk Bookfair is a micro bookfair organized by Birdcall and Ningen Paper Press.

KR



Dates

2026.12.12.Saturday - 12.13.Sunday

12 - 7pm



Location

Birdcall

B1, 25 Jahamunro 19gil Jongro-gu, Seoul, Korea



Organized by

Birdcall and Ningen Paper Press



How?

  1. Send a physical copy of a book you want to submit with your email address written on a piece of paper by 11/30(Mon) to the address below:
    Desk Book Fair(+82-2272-2318)
    B1, 25 Jahamunro 19gil Jongro-gu, Seoul, Korea
  2. Orgaznizers of Desk Bookfair select ±50 number of books by looking at the physical books.
  3. If your book is selected, you will receive an email notification with further information.
  4. We do not return the unselected books.
  5. The participation fee is 30,000KRW per book, and each publisher can participate with up to three books.
  6. There is no additional sales fee other than the participation fee.


Why?

  1. To simplify the application, promotion, operation, design processes and the participation fee
  2. To try receiving and selecting submissions of physical books rather than proposals
  3. To have a long conversation on publishing with small number of people
  4. To present Birdcall and Ningen Paper Press's standards of good books
  5. To question on planning and publishing new books upon the book fair appication
  6. To share the experiences from other book fairs
